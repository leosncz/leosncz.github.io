Questions & Answers

What happens if I'm not satisfied? +

Your first scan is completely free. If you decide to subscribe, you can cancel anytime within the first 30 days with no charges. We're confident in our results, but if we're not right for you, there's no penalty.

How long does a scan take? +

The initial scan runs in about 15 minutes after you enter your domain. Full results are delivered within 24 hours. After that, weekly scans run automatically every Monday morning.

Do you need access to my systems? +

No. We only need your company domain. We scan 150+ public breach databases and darkweb forums. No VPN, no infrastructure access, no API integrations required to start. Optional integrations come later if you want them.

What if an employee left months ago? +

We'll still alert you if their credentials appear in a breach. Former employees often reuse passwords at new companies. Compromised credentials are a liability either way. The dashboard lets you filter by current/former status.

Can I cancel mid-month? +

Yes. Cancel anytime and you won't be charged for the next cycle. No long-term contract, no early termination fees. If you cancel during your first 30 days, you pay nothing at all.

What's included in the CEO call? +

A 15-minute monthly call with our CEO to review your breach findings, discuss your security posture, answer questions, and help you prioritize fixes. It's tactical, not sales. You can reschedule if needed.

How do compliance reports work? +

We auto-generate reports for GDPR, ISO 27001, and SOC 2 audits. They show when breaches were detected, which employees were affected, and what steps you took to remediate. Everything is timestamped for audit trails.

Can I integrate with our SIEM? +

Yes. The Complete Plan includes API access. You can pull scan data, set up webhooks, and integrate with Slack, Teams, or custom tools. Enterprise plans support deeper integrations with your security stack.
