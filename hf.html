Questions

Answers before you start

Do you scan our employees' personal accounts? +

No. We only check the professional email address (firstname.lastname@yourcompany.com) against third party breach data. We never access personal inboxes, personal social accounts, or any data outside of what a breach has already exposed about that work address.

Where is our data hosted? +

Entirely in France and the EU. Scanning, storage and processing all stay on European infrastructure. No transfer outside the EU and no non European subprocessor in the chain.

How long does the first scan take? +

The scan itself runs in about 15 minutes. An engineer then reviews the results and delivers a report within 24 hours.

Do you need access to our systems? +

No. We only need your company domain. No VPN, no infrastructure access, no mandatory integrations. Optional SIEM integrations come later if you want them.

What about employees who have already left? +

We still flag them. Former employees reuse passwords at new companies, so their exposure remains a liability. The dashboard lets you filter by current or former status.

Can I cancel? +

Yes, anytime. No long term contract and no early termination fee. Nothing is charged during the free trial scan.

How do compliance reports work? +

We auto generate timestamped reports for GDPR, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 audits, showing when each breach was detected and what remediation steps were taken.

Can I integrate with our SIEM? +

Yes. The Complete Plan includes API access and webhooks for Slack, Teams or custom tooling.

Who reviews my report? +

A named engineer, identified by matricule number on every report you receive, and reachable directly by reply if you have questions about a finding.

I would rather talk to someone before scanning anything, is that possible? +

The free trial scan is the fastest way to see what we do before committing to anything. If you would still like to talk it through first, you can reach our CEO directly.

Our story

Who is Heimflux?

Heimflux was created by Léo SANCHEZ, a DevSecOps engineer with over a decade of experience defending infrastructure and teams at scale. What started as frustration became a mission.

Léo spent years responding to breaches that should never have happened. Accounts compromised for months before anyone noticed. Employees using weak passwords from the 2013 Adobe breach in their corporate email. Attackers with customer data, internal emails, sometimes even source code, because a single compromised account opened the door.

The worst part? Companies weren't even checking. They'd run compliance scans, gather vulnerability reports, but nobody was systematically asking: "Are our employees' credentials already for sale on the dark web?"

That changed with Heimflux. We built a service that answers one question: Who in your organization is already compromised? Not theories. Not predictions. Real accounts, real breaches, real action. An engineer reviews every finding to cut the noise. France-based infrastructure to keep data safe.

One mission: Stop reactive security. Start being proactive.
