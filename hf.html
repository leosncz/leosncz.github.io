Questions

Before you book the call.

What if you don't find anything critical?+
Then your report says so. A quiet week is still a reviewed week. You get the full control list, the score, and confirmation that nothing needs your attention. That is the point: you stop wondering.
Does HeimFlux replace our IT provider or MSP?+
No. HeimFlux is a monitoring and review layer, not a helpdesk or an infrastructure manager. We tell you what is wrong and why it matters, your MSP or internal IT still implements the fix, unless you would like us to advise directly on remediation.
What access do you need to our Microsoft 365 tenant?+
Read only, scoped to the identity, endpoint and configuration data needed for the checks listed in the Coverage section. We never request admin credentials or write access.
Who actually reviews the findings?+
A member of our security engineering team, not an algorithm. Every report shows the name of the engineer who reviewed it, and you can reply directly to ask them anything.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes. Your first month is free, so you see real reports before anything is billed. Cancel in one click before month two and you pay nothing. After that, it is a monthly subscription you can cancel anytime.
Do you cover environments beyond Microsoft 365?+
Today, coverage focuses on Microsoft 365 and Entra ID, where most SMB incidents originate. Support for AWS, VMware and on premises environments is coming, get in touch if you need a specific region or a dedicated deployment now.
Where is our data stored?+
Hosted in France, data stays in the EU. If you need a specific region or full isolation, we can deploy a dedicated environment.
